# Legal

## No game files, ever

NanoCraft distributes **launcher scripts, a framebuffer presenter, a quick menu,
a software OpenGL stack and a bundled C runtime**. It contains:

- no Minecraft APK,
- no `libminecraftpe.so`,
- no Minecraft assets, textures, sounds or data,
- no worlds or save files.

None of those will ever be added. The game comes from an APK **you** supply,
from a copy **you** own. NanoCraft extracts it, unmodified, on your own device.

## What this project does not do

- It does not mirror, host or link to game files.
- It does not defeat, bypass or weaken any technical protection measure.
- It does not provide any way to obtain Minecraft without owning it.
- It does not patch, repack or modify the game library. Ninecraft loads your
  original `libminecraftpe.so` exactly as extracted.

## Your APK

Obtaining Minecraft Pocket Edition 0.8.1 is your responsibility, and what is
lawful depends on where you live and what you own. This project takes no
position on it beyond the obvious: **use your own copy, and do not redistribute
it.**

Some 0.8.1 downloads in circulation are repackaged "wrapper" APKs. NanoCraft can
extract those because that is the shape most copies take, not as an endorsement
of any particular source.
